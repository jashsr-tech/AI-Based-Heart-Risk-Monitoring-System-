  * Conduction Blocks (LBBB & RBBB): Wide QRS complexes cause fixed window overlap in
    LBBB (54/99 beats, 54.5%), distorting ST-segment calculation.
  * CONCLUSION: Fixed [R-120ms, R-80ms] baseline estimation is fundamentally invalid for
    pathological ECGs. The validated dynamic baseline (between P-offset and Q-onset) with
    flatness verification successfully avoids contamination and flags invalid baselines as NaN.

  * Median separation                      : 36.1 ms
  * 5th to 95th percentile range           : [13.9 ms, 86.1 ms]
  * CLINICAL PROOF: The J-point (QRS offset) occurs 20 ms to 80 ms AFTER the S-peak nadir.
    Treating S-peak as the J-point introduces severe timing distortion (20-80 ms error).
    Evaluating ST-amplitude at (S + 60ms) mistakenly samples inside the late QRS upslope.
    Evaluating ST-amplitude at (J + 60ms) accurately captures the true ST segment.
  * GATING CERTIFICATION: ST amplitude is evaluated ONLY when both J-point and isoelectric
    baseline are certified valid; otherwise marked NaN.
